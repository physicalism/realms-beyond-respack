Custom decorations for Minecraft Java 26.3.

- v0.1.45: Existing Large Forge displays now show the red-brick Smouldering Forge with an animated fire grate and smoking chimneys. Use `realm:large_forge_animated` for the previous molten forge.

- Added Claude’s revised hanging Blacksmith sign, with full-bright lettering and an anvil emblem, a recessed timber frame, and crossed chains.
- Added the Origin Realms Large Forge decoration, with animated molten channels, glowing piston shafts, and a heated sword tip.

- v0.1.43: Connected 3D dirt paths (needs Fusion): paths sit 1px below the lawn with organic, rounded, pixel-art edges; lawn sides show path edging only under path surface; no gaps or floating edges next to slabs, stairs and crops. Sparse simple stones dotted randomly across grass (vanilla, no mod). Petal chainsaw and lightning bolt cosmetics. Corner balcony flowers are now orange; refined stag fountain stag.

- v0.1.42: Added two Lucky Ivy figurines with an animated beckoning paw, in gold and in Ivy’s black coat. Use `realm:lucky_ivy` or `realm:lucky_ivy_coloured` in an invisible floor-mounted item frame.

- v0.1.41: Added a mossy stone fountain with a bronze stag, four water spouts, and animated splash foam. The README includes placement commands. Adjusted the ceiling-mounted dreamcatcher offset to clear the chest.

- 96 banners: lion, dragon, and stag designs in all 16 dye colours, with gold or brown finishes.
- Balcony flowers: pink petals, a yellow ribbon, and trailing green leaves, with corrected corner placement.
- Four Ivy portraits: original, crown, detailed crown, and Tudor library.
- Solid wooden painting frames with full canvas coverage.
- Custom pack icon and formatted version and author information.
- Vanilla item models for invisible wall-mounted item frames; no CIT mod required.

Painting commands are listed in the README.

- Independent balcony flower corner variant with light blue petals and a yellow ribbon.

- Normal lily pads use plain, pink, purple, or white lotus models with equal 25% probabilities and varied rotations.

- Added pale tropical aqua and yellow lotuses; six equally likely pad types, with an original-four switch available.

- v0.1.40: Added a farm scarecrow with a tilted pumpkin head, a straw hat, and a faded rust-red hat band. Use `realm:scarecrow` in a floor-mounted invisible item frame or an item display. Existing placements using `realm:scarecrow_farm_1` still work.

- v0.1.39: Ivy's back now slopes down from her shoulders in two steps instead of meeting her body at a sharp right angle, on both the Ivy shrine and the stone Ivy shrine. Item IDs are unchanged.

- v0.1.38: Ivy shrine revision. Slimmer hind legs with rounded haunches and a rounded rump, a fuller chest that comes forward to the collar tag, a thicker tail that drapes over the back of the plinth (no longer animated), toe lines on the hind paws to match the front paws, and a plain emerald collar. Fixed z-fighting on the tail. Applies to both the Ivy shrine and the stone Ivy shrine; item IDs are unchanged.
