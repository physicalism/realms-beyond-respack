Custom decorations for Minecraft Java 26.3.

- v0.1.57 (14 November 2026):
  - Added sparse ivory, coral pink, and orange clam shells, occasional pebbles, and very rare starfish to ordinary sand, using vanilla random block models (shells approximately 1 in 167 blocks; starfish 1 in 2000).
  - Added larger grey and pale ivory shell-like pebbles; preserved starfish appearance and rarity.
  - Included the latest Elphaba hat and broom assets.
  - Added the project-wide art direction guide and complete illustrated source catalogue.
  - Fixed missing particle texture references on Blacksmith, Florist, and Clocktower signs.
  - Includes all existing kitchen, barrel tap, Chef Ivy, cake, and connected terrain changes from v0.1.56.

- v0.1.56 (13 November 2026):
  - Added animated kitchen decorations: bread cutting, a vegetable frying pan with flames, and a rotating spit.
  - Added the barrel tap model, used by unattached tripwire hooks; attached hooks retain their vanilla models.
  - Added the Chef Ivy painting.
  - Refined Ivy’s cake texture and inventory icon, with a 3D cake model in hand.
  - Updated connected path and farmland edges, added surrounding dirt variants, and optimised their Fusion conditions.
  - Refined Florist sign lettering spacing and updated the cosmetics player configuration.

- v0.1.55:
  - Replaced the vanilla cake with the Ivy cake: a black Labrador topper of Ivy with fruit, flowers and icing, adapted from Hoffen's (@_InkGhoul) Jellie cake. All seven bite states keep vanilla bounds, toppings come off the cut side, and Ivy falls into the eaten area after the fifth bite. Candle cakes and the inventory icon are included.

- v0.1.51:
  - Added the Florist sign (`realm:florist_sign`), a hanging sign in the same style as the Blacksmith and Clocktower signs, with a five-petal flower emblem and `FLORIST` in capitals. Summon it like the other signs, tagged `rb_florist_sign`; the command is in the README.

- v0.1.49:
  - Added the anvil hammer (`realm:anvil_hammer`): an animated blacksmith's hammer that strikes a vanilla anvil and throws glowing sparks. Put it in an invisible item frame on top of the anvil.
  - Added the blacksmith hammer (`realm:blacksmith_hammer`): the same hammer as a plain item to hold or to put in an NPC's hand.
  - Commands for both are in the README.

- v0.1.48:
  - Added the hanging stag banner (`realm:banner_stag_hanging_<dye>`) in all 16 dye colours: a small animated banner whose pole runs straight out from the end of a trapdoor and ends in a gold spike shaped like the Blacksmith sign's bracket. Summon commands are in the README.
  - The Royal Stag war banner now comes in all 16 dye colours (`realm:banner_stag_war_<dye>`) as well as azure, with a pixel-matched pole and finials.
  - The Blacksmith and Clocktower signs now use capitals (`BLACKSMITH`, `CLOCKTOWER`), and their full-bright lettering no longer turns dark or flickers when seen from a distance.

- v0.1.47:
  - Added the Clocktower sign (`realm:town_clocktower_sign`), a wider version of the Blacksmith sign with a clock emblem. Summon it like the Blacksmith sign, tagged `rb_clocktower_sign`.
  - Added the Royal Stag war banner (`realm:banner_stag_war_azure`), a long azure banner with a gold border, crown, outlined stag, fleur-de-lis, dagged hem with tassels, a gently swaying animation and a spruce pole. Place it in an invisible item frame on the wall block under a beam.
  - Cosmetics clean-up: the pack now only carries the cosmetics player list (`wardrobe.properties`: Henry's chainsaw, face drip, footprints and hand drips, and maxsmell's wizard hat). All cosmetic art now comes from the Realms Beyond Cosmetics mod (v0.1.32+), so the pack can no longer override it with older chainsaw or lightning textures. The old footprint-datapack models are removed.

- v0.1.46: Removed the Large Forge, Smouldering Forge and Claude's Forge decorations. Neighbouring banners no longer flicker where their poles overlap.

- Added Claude’s revised hanging Blacksmith sign, with full-bright lettering and an anvil emblem, a recessed timber frame, and crossed chains.

- v0.1.43: Connected 3D dirt paths (needs Fusion): paths sit 1px below the lawn with organic, rounded, pixel-art edges; lawn sides show path edging only under path surface; no gaps or floating edges next to slabs, stairs and crops. Sparse simple stones dotted randomly across grass (vanilla, no mod). Petal chainsaw and lightning bolt cosmetics. Corner balcony flowers are now orange; refined stag fountain stag.

- v0.1.42: Added two Lucky Ivy figurines with an animated beckoning paw, in gold and in Ivy’s black coat. Use `realm:lucky_ivy` or `realm:lucky_ivy_coloured` in an invisible floor-mounted item frame.
